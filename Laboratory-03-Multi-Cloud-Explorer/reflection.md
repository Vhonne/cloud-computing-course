# Mission Reflection

The cloud platform that impressed me the most is Google Cloud Platform (GCP). I found its services for Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Kubernetes interesting because they can support modern applications and research projects. I also learned that each cloud provider has different strengths depending on what an organization needs.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide cloud computing services such as virtual machines, storage, networking, databases, identity management, and security services. They also allow organizations to access resources through the cloud and scale their infrastructure based on their requirements.

Business requirements are important when selecting a cloud platform because organizations have different technologies, budgets, and goals. For example, a university that already uses Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory may benefit more from Azure. An AI research company may prefer GCP because of its AI and Machine Learning capabilities, while a startup may choose AWS because of its wide range of available services.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about choosing the most popular platform. I learned to consider the client's existing environment, technical requirements, budget, scalability, and business objectives before making a recommendation. This helped me understand how technical decisions can support business needs.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved after completing this mission because I added organized documentation about AWS, Azure, and GCP. I also created comparison tables, client recommendations, and screenshots as evidence of my work. Overall, this mission improved my understanding of multi-cloud environments and helped me practice presenting technical information in an organized way.
